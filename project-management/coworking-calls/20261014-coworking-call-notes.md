## Notes

Journal Club Agenda:

We're joined by Dr Doudou BA to talk about "Building an Autonomous AI Data Scientist", with his tool OctOpus being case study: [https://octoopus.dev/](https://octoopus.dev/)

   * Data Science answers the question of "what next" after AI. 
   * OctOpus understands your data based on your dataset structure and its quality, chooses a model, and trains the model based on it.  
       * It uses a stack of models.
       * It has the ability to process speech.
       * It also has a "brain" that can be turned on at will. 
   * There's also a marketplace to enable users to sell the models they've trained.
   * "You ask the question, OctOpus does the work."
