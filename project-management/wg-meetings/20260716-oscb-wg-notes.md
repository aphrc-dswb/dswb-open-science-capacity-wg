## Notes

### Open Science and Communicating Research

**Research is not finished unless it is communicated.**

Few African studies are published: about 3% of global research output from last year.

Most of the work we publish is done outside Africa.

### What Is Open Science?

Open Science means making research:

- **Accessible:** Anyone can access the research outputs and data.
- **Transparent:** Methods, data, and processes are open and clear.
- **Reproducible:** Others can verify and reproduce the results.
  - If someone else uses the same parameters to carry out my research, they should be able to get similar results.
- **Reusable:** Data and outputs can be reused for new research.
- **Collaborative:** Researchers work together across institutions, disciplines, and borders.

### Benefits of Open Science

- Increased citations
- Increased visibility
- More collaborations
- Greater societal impact
- Accountability and trust

**Open access papers enable access.**

#### Tip

Budget for open access publication fees from the beginning of the research process.

### Open Science Research Life Cycle

**1. Research Idea**

- Define the problem.
- Review the literature.
- Identify your audience.

**2. Data Collection**

- Plan ethically.
- Document clearly.
- Communicate methods.

**3. Data Management**

- Organize data.
- Store data securely.
- Share data using **FAIR principles**.

**4. Analysis**

- Analyze rigorously.
- Keep records transparent.
- Ensure the research is reproducible.

**5. Manuscript Writing**

- Write clearly.
- Tell the story.
- Cite responsibly.

**6. Publication**

- Choose the right journal.
- Make your work openly accessible.

**7. Dissemination**

- Share widely.
- Present.
- Publish.
- Post.
- Engage different audiences.

**8. Impact**

- Inform policy.
- Improve practice.
- Contribute to society.

### Writing Research That Gets Read

**Focus on Clarity, Not Complexity**

- Make the research easy to understand.
- Avoid unnecessary jargon.
- Communicate the key finding clearly.

**Understand Peer Review**

- Understand how peer review works.
- Anticipate reviewers' questions.
- Respond constructively to feedback.

**Be Transparent About AI**

- Be transparent about your use of AI in your research and writing.
- **Declare your use of AI where required.**
